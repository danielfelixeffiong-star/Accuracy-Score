Privacy Policy

Application: Accuracy Score • Last Updated: August 20, 2026
Sports Analytics & Informational Service: Accuracy Score provides football statistical analysis, prediction models, and match information strictly for entertainment and informational purposes. Accuracy Score is not a gambling operator and does not facilitate real-money wagering.
1. Introduction

Welcome to Accuracy Score ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Accuracy Score mobile application and associated web interfaces.

2. Information We Collect

Technical & Diagnostic Data: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information (such as device model, OS version, and performance logs) is processed locally to optimize display responsiveness.

Permissions We DO NOT Access: Accuracy Score does not request or access sensitive device features:

  • No access to Contacts or Address Book
  • No access to Microphone or Audio inputs
  • No access to Camera or Photos
  • No access to GPS Location
  • No access to SMS or Call Logs
  • No access to Local Storage Files

3. Firebase & Google Cloud Storage

Accuracy Score uses Google Cloud Firestore to store and synchronize sports analysis and predictions in real time. All data transmissions between the application and Google Cloud servers are encrypted using standard transport-layer security (HTTPS/TLS).

4. Firebase Authentication

Firebase Authentication is utilized strictly for authorized administrators to manage and publish match tips. Ordinary users browsing free predictions, correct scores, VIP analysis, and notifications are not required to register or provide login credentials.

5. Application Data & Synchronized Content

Content synchronized via Firestore includes:

  • Free Tips: Daily football predictions for Today, Yesterday, and Tomorrow.
  • VIP Matches: Match fixtures, odds, and confidence ratings.
  • Correct Score Results: Match status settlements (Won, Lost, Void) and historical archives.
  • Notifications: Broadcast alerts and match schedule announcements.
  • App Settings: Official support URLs and disclaimer notices.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Accuracy Score may use Google AdMob or other advertising networks in future updates. If advertising is enabled, this policy will be updated to describe the information collected, advertising identifiers, and ad personalization. Advertising SDKs are not actively collecting user advertising identifiers in this version.

7. Payments & Subscriptions

If VIP subscriptions are introduced through Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchases, transactions will be processed directly through the respective platform. Accuracy Score does not directly process, collect, or store users' credit or debit card details.

9. Data Security

We implement industry-standard technical measures, including HTTPS/TLS encryption and Firestore Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), to safeguard application data. While we take rigorous precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% infallible.

10. Data Retention

Information stored in Firestore (such as historical match records) is retained only for as long as necessary to serve the informational and historical tracking purposes of Accuracy Score.

11. Account & Data Deletion Rights

Because standard users are not required to create accounts or personal profiles, the application does not feature an in-app deletion button. For any privacy requests or administrative inquiries, you may email us at support@accuracyscore.site.

12. Children's Privacy (Age Limit 18+)

Accuracy Score is not directed at children under the age of 13. Users who do not meet the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction (such as 18 years of age) should not use the service.
