Is Accuracy Score free to use?

The app includes free football picks and published results. Optional weekly and monthly VIP subscriptions unlock additional content. Eligible free accounts may see ads.

Can I download it on Android and iPhone?

Accuracy Score is available on Android through Google Play. The iOS version is coming soon; an App Store download link will be added when it is available.

Does Accuracy Score accept bets?

No. Accuracy Score provides football predictions and analysis. It does not accept bets, hold betting funds or award gambling prizes. Picks are opinions, and no result or profit is guaranteed. The service is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

How do I receive match updates?

Allow notifications when prompted in the app. Published updates can then appear on your phone. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

Where can I get account or subscription help?

Use Contact Admin in the app or visit our support page. Manage or cancel an Android subscription in Google Play. Read our privacy policy for information about your data.
